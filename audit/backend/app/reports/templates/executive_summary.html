About Post-Quantum Cryptography

Quantum computers running Shor's algorithm will break RSA and elliptic-curve cryptography. While no cryptographically relevant quantum computer exists today, adversaries are collecting encrypted traffic now for future decryption (Harvest-Now Decrypt-Later). NIST finalised post-quantum standards in August 2024. India's DST Task Force published a migration roadmap in February 2026. Migration typically takes 42–54 months — organisations should begin now.

Methodology & Provenance

Tool: SeQr scanner — sslyze 6.0 for passive TLS analysis and an OpenSSL 3.5+ ML-KEM ClientHello for active post-quantum detection (passive scanning alone cannot confirm hybrid PQC support).

Method: passive measurement plus an active key-exchange probe. No authentication, no exploitation, no data exfiltration — equivalent to a single ordinary client connection, rate-limited.

Coverage: TLS (443), SSH key exchange + host keys (22), mail STARTTLS (SMTP / IMAP / POP3), and database TLS (PostgreSQL).

Scan confidence: high = direct connection; medium = cloud-provider IP; low = CDN-terminated (origin configuration is not externally observable).

Not assessed: origin servers behind CDNs, application-layer data handling, and internal / non-public services. Assessment date: {{ generated_at }}.
