# PRODUCT.md — SeQr

register: brand

## Product purpose
SeQr measures the cryptographic posture of financial infrastructure and turns it into an
audit-ready inventory (a CBOM) mapped to Indian regulation. It detects what standard scanners
miss: passive tools report 0% post-quantum adoption while active probing reveals 60%, and that
60% is a CDN-edge mirage sitting on 100% classical certificates and exposed origins. The
landing page must make a skeptical security buyer feel the gap between "we look modern" and
"we are quietly quantum-exposed."

## Users
- **Primary:** CISO / Head of Information Security / CTO at Indian fintechs, brokerages,
  payment aggregators, NBFCs, insurers. Technical, skeptical of vendor hype, allergic to
  fluff, under real regulatory pressure (PCI DSS 12.3.3 + SEBI CSCRF are live mandates).
- **Secondary:** compliance/GRC leads who need the CBOM as audit evidence; security
  researchers who respect the measurement work.

## Brand voice
Three words: **precise, adversarial, sovereign.**
- Precise: every number is measured, never inferred. Show the evidence.
- Adversarial: names an uncomfortable truth the buyer's current tools hide. Not alarmist,
  not FUD; the calm confidence of someone holding the data.
- Sovereign: made in India, for Indian regulation and data-residency. Not a US tool with
  CNSA 2.0 boilerplate.

Tone: an instrument, not a salesperson. It reports a signal. Confident, terse, technical.

## Anti-references (what to avoid)
- Neon-on-black "crypto/quantum" cliche (glowing circuits, matrix rain, hexagons).
- Navy-and-gold "fintech trust" cliche.
- Lattice/wireframe-globe WebGL (brittle, never renders well, overused).
- SaaS hero-metric template (huge number, small label, three stat cards, gradient accent).
- Generic three-icon "how it works" card grids.
- FUD ("QUANTUM APOCALYPSE"), fake countdown timers, doomsday copy.

## Strategic principles
- Lead with the research finding (0% vs 60% mirage) — it is the hook and the proof.
- Frameworks are accurate and dated (PCI DSS 12.3.3 live; SEBI CSCRF live; DST 2027 is a
  recommendatory milestone, not a filing deadline; DPDP enforceable 2027-05-13). Never
  overstate. See docs/09.
- The product is a scanner today; the strategy is scanner -> inventory -> migrate. Marketing
  may hint at the migration bridge but must not claim capabilities not shipped.
- Company is "SeQr Labs"; product/scanner is "SeQr".
